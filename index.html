Frequently Asked Questions

Is Xoni free to use?

Xoni offers free messaging, audio calls, and video calls. Internet connectivity is required, and your network provider may charge for data usage.

How do I create an account?

Open Xoni and follow the registration instructions. Complete the required account and human verification steps.

Why does Xoni require human verification?

Human verification is designed to discourage automated and fake accounts and encourage genuine interactions. It does not guarantee the identity or intentions of other users.

What if my messages or calls aren't working?

Check your internet connection, confirm that Xoni has the necessary microphone and camera permissions, and restart the app. If the problem continues, contact support.

How do I block another user?

Use Xoni's contact management and blocking features to manage unwanted connections.

How can I delete my account?

Use the account deletion option in Xoni's settings. If you cannot access your account, contact support for assistance.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@xonerapp.com with a description of the issue, your device type, and relevant screenshots if available. Never include passwords or verification codes.

Privacy and Safety

We want Xoni to be a welcoming environment for genuine communication. If you experience inappropriate behavior or have concerns about your account, please contact our support team.
