Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the information Xoni processes, why it is used, how it is shared, how long it is retained and the controls available to you.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Xoni and explains how personal information is processed when you create an account, use Human Verification, manage your profile, send messages, make calls or otherwise use the service.

Contact: contact@xonerapp.com.

2. Information Xoni processes

Account information may include your email address, authentication status, internal user ID, Xoni ID, username, account timestamps and secure device-session information.

Profile information may include your username, profile photo, availability status and custom status.

Communications data may include message content, message sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, read status, call participants, call type, call status, call timestamps and technical signaling data needed to establish internet calls.

Human Verification data includes verification-session identifiers, randomized challenge sequences, completion status and timestamps. In the current implementation, the live camera feed is used on the device to perform the movement checks and the backend receives the verification result/session data rather than a stored verification video.

Technical and security information may include authentication tokens, session identifiers, request metadata and information needed to prevent abuse, secure accounts and operate the service.

3. Camera and microphone

Camera access is used for Human Verification and for video-call functionality. Microphone access is used for audio and video calls and other audio features that you choose to use.

Xoni does not use camera or microphone access for unrelated purposes.

4. Google and Apple sign-in

If you choose Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, Xoni receives authentication information from that provider, including a provider-issued identity token and the account information made available through the selected sign-in flow.

Xoni uses this information to authenticate the account and does not receive your Google or Apple password.

5. How Xoni uses information

Xoni uses personal information to create and authenticate accounts; provide Human Verification; provide profiles, messaging and calling; maintain sessions and presence; deliver requested service functions; troubleshoot errors; prevent abuse and fraud; secure the service; respond to reports; enforce the Terms; and comply with legal obligations.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on performance of the service requested by you, legitimate interests in operating and securing Xoni, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where consent is required.

6. Service providers

Xoni currently uses Render for backend hosting and database infrastructure, Cloudinary for profile-photo storage, Resend for transactional email delivery, Google and Apple for optional authentication, and Cloudflare TURN infrastructure to help establish internet calls when direct peer-to-peer connectivity is not available.

These providers process information only as needed to provide their respective services and are subject to their own privacy and security terms.

7. Messages, calls and media

Message content is stored by Xoni so conversations can be delivered and displayed.

Xoni stores call metadata and signaling information needed to establish and manage calls. Xoni does not intentionally record or store the audio or video content of ordinary Xoni calls as part of the current call service.

Call media may pass through peer-to-peer or TURN infrastructure as technically required to connect participants.

8. Profile photos

If you upload a profile photo, it is stored through Xoni's media-storage provider and associated with your account. You can replace it, and it is removed as part of account deletion where technically available.

9. Sharing and disclosure

Xoni may disclose information to service providers that process data on Xoni's behalf, to comply with legal obligations, to respond to lawful requests, to protect users and the service, or in connection with a legitimate business restructuring.

Xoni does not sell personal information to advertisers.

10. International processing

Xoni is a global communications service. Information may therefore be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, Xoni will use appropriate transfer mechanisms.

11. Data retention

Account, profile, message, call and session information is retained while reasonably necessary to provide Xoni, maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

Unused or expired verification sessions and authentication records may be deleted or cleaned up as part of normal service operation.

When you delete your account, Xoni's current backend deletes the account and associated database records linked to it and removes the stored profile photo where one exists. Limited records may be retained only where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or enforcement.

12. Account deletion

You can initiate permanent account deletion from within Xoni. Google Play also requires an external web resource for account-deletion requests; Xoni will provide that public deletion page for the store listing.

Deleting an account is intended to delete the account rather than merely freeze or hide it.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

To request assistance, email contact@xonerapp.com.

14. Security

Xoni uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect accounts and service data, including secure session tokens, server-side authentication, transport encryption and access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

15. Children and age-related protections

Xoni must be used in accordance with the age requirements and restrictions that apply in the country and app store through which the service is made available. Xoni does not knowingly permit unlawful exploitation or abuse of children and prohibits child sexual abuse material and related conduct.

16. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Xoni's features, providers, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Xoni can be sent to contact@xonerapp.com.

For general app support, visit Xoni Support or email support@xonerapp.com.
